Brooklyn towing built for city conditions

New York towing is rarely simple. Tight parking, active traffic lanes, damaged vehicles and limited loading space can all change how a vehicle should be moved. JB Towing & Recovery uses a practical, vehicle-first approach to help safely load and transport vehicles throughout Brooklyn.

Service coverage includes Brooklyn neighborhoods including Williamsburg, Bushwick, Bed-Stuy, Crown Heights, Flatbush, Bay Ridge, Sunset Park, Canarsie and Coney Island. Availability can vary based on pickup conditions, vehicle size and destination, so the fastest way to confirm service is to call with the exact location and vehicle details.

Flatbed towing in Brooklyn

Flatbed towing is a strong option for all-wheel-drive vehicles, lowered cars, SUVs, vehicles with collision damage and any vehicle that should remain fully off the road during transport. The vehicle is loaded onto the bed and secured for the trip.

Accident and damaged-vehicle recovery

If the vehicle has wheel, suspension or body damage, tell us before dispatch. Sharing whether the vehicle rolls, steers and shifts into neutral helps determine the safest loading approach.

SUV, pickup and commercial vehicle transport

JB Towing & Recovery’s work includes SUVs, pickups and select commercial vehicles. Provide the make, model, approximate size and current condition when calling so equipment compatibility can be confirmed.

Need a tow from Brooklyn?
