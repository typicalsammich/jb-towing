Staten Island towing built for city conditions

New York towing is rarely simple. Tight parking, active traffic lanes, damaged vehicles and limited loading space can all change how a vehicle should be moved. JB Towing & Recovery uses a practical, vehicle-first approach to help safely load and transport vehicles throughout Staten Island.

Service coverage includes Staten Island neighborhoods and routes connecting to Brooklyn and the rest of New York City. Availability can vary based on pickup conditions, vehicle size and destination, so the fastest way to confirm service is to call with the exact location and vehicle details.

Flatbed towing in Staten Island

Flatbed towing is a strong option for all-wheel-drive vehicles, lowered cars, SUVs, vehicles with collision damage and any vehicle that should remain fully off the road during transport. The vehicle is loaded onto the bed and secured for the trip.

Accident and damaged-vehicle recovery

If the vehicle has wheel, suspension or body damage, tell us before dispatch. Sharing whether the vehicle rolls, steers and shifts into neutral helps determine the safest loading approach.
